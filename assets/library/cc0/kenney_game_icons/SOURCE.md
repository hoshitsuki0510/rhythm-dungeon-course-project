## Retained content

The complete original archive is retained byte-for-byte under `original/`.
To keep the working tree small, `content/` contains only the 16 selected 1x
black and white PNG icons needed or likely useful for the current prototype:
`arrowDown`, `arrowLeft`, `arrowRight`, `arrowUp`, `checkmark`, `cross`,
`musicOff`, `musicOn`, `pause`, `plus`, `signal1`, `signal2`, `signal3`,
`star`, `target`, and `warning` (each in both color variants), plus the original
`license.txt`. The unexpanded archive remains available if another icon is
needed later.

## Authorization conclusion

The official page labels this package **Creative Commons CC0**. The embedded
`content/license.txt` says the graphics may be used in personal and commercial
projects and that credit is nice but not mandatory. The selected files and the
retained source archive may therefore be modified and publicly redistributed in
this educational GitHub repository.

## Verification and risk

The archive hash and byte count above were measured immediately after download;
`unzip -t` passed. The selected PNGs were copied without pixel edits; their
source paths are preserved in the archive. The only residual risk is choosing
an icon scale/color that clashes with the eventual pixel-art palette, which is
a visual integration decision rather than a licensing issue.
