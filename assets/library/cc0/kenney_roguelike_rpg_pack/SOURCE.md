## Retained content

The archive is retained byte-for-byte under `original/`. Its contents are
expanded under `content/`, including the original `License.txt`, 16x16
roguelike spritesheet (transparent and magenta variants), spritesheet metadata,
sample forest and indoor maps, and preview/sample images. The package provides
forest ground, trees, plants, paths, structures, and dungeon interiors useful
for later room dressing.
